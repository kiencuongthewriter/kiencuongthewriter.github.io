Kien Cuong is a Vietnamese comic book writer living in Singapore. He works full-time as an engineer and lives part-time dreading his job as an engineer. He spends time taking care of his indoor sweet potato and binge-reading Grant Morrison, Junji Ito, Mark Russel’s most obscure works. He mostly feels sad, and when he feels too much, he writes.
